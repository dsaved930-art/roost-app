Not permitted on Roost
  • Eagles of any species — private ownership/transfer is essentially never permitted under federal law
  • Wild-caught native bird species — captive-bred only
  • Species listed under the Endangered Species Act or CITES Appendix I
  • Any species you don't legally own or can't legally transfer
